# Literature review

Every entry was checked against arXiv or Crossref (DOI resolves to the stated title) on 2026‑10‑05. Numbers from papers are quoted only where they were checked. Otherwise the entry describes what was done, not how well.

"Ours" refers to this project: **DA‑CRN**, a causal, degradation‑aware convolutional recurrent network for joint denoising and dereverberation at 16 kHz (see `docs/architecture.md`).

## 5. Take‑aways that drove our design

1. **Real‑time constraint.** Causal CRN‑family models (CRN, DCCRN, CRUSE, GTCRN, DeepFilterNet) are the established real‑time family. Time‑domain separators (Conv‑TasNet, DPRNN) and GAN/BLSTM models (SEGAN, MetricGAN+, MP‑SENet) are either non‑causal or too heavy.
2. **Phase matters for reverberation.** Late reverb smears phase, so magnitude‑only masks (CRN, NSNet2) are limited. A bounded complex ratio mask (DCCRN) is a cheap fix.
3. **Joint noise + reverb.** Two‑stage systems (Zhao et al.) work but double compute. T60‑aware dereverb (Wu et al.) shows that knowing the acoustic condition helps. Noise‑aware training (Seltzer; Xu) shows the same for noise. No single compact causal model in this list conditions on *both* online SNR and reverberation estimates. That is the gap our contribution tests.
4. **Losses.** SI‑SDR (Le Roux) for waveform fidelity plus multi‑resolution STFT (Yamamoto; used by Demucs) for spectral fidelity is a common, well‑behaved combination. We ablate the STFT term.
5. **Evaluation.** PESQ/STOI/SI‑SDR per condition (WHAMR! style), plus ASR WER (REVERB style) and non‑intrusive DNSMOS (DNS style) for real recordings.
